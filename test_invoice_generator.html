📝 How to Use

For Purchase Invoices:

  1. Go to Purchases page
  2. Find any purchase record
  3. Click the green 📄 FileText icon in the Actions column
  4. PDF will automatically download!

For Sale Invoices:

  1. Go to Crop Sales page
  2. Find any sale record
  3. Click the green 📄 FileText icon in the Actions column
  4. PDF will automatically download!
